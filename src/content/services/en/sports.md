## What to expect

Sports medicine visits focus on what you want to do, not just what's hurting. We'll examine the injury, image as needed, and build a plan oriented around your sport, your season and your goals.

## Our approach

Athletes need plans that account for sport demands, training cycles and team commitments. We coordinate care with your trainers, coaches and physical therapists to build seamless return-to-play programs — supported by objective testing that confirms you're ready before you return to competition.
