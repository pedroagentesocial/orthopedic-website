## What to expect

Shoulder and elbow visits include a complete range-of-motion exam, strength testing, and — when indicated — diagnostic ultrasound or MRI to clarify the source of pain or weakness.

## Our approach

Most shoulder problems respond to a targeted physical therapy program combined with image-guided injections. When surgery is required, arthroscopic techniques allow us to repair tendons and stabilize joints through small incisions, with most patients home the same day and back to driving within two weeks.
