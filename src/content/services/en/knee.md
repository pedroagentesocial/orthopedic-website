## What to expect

Most knee and hip evaluations begin with a 45-minute visit. We'll review your symptom history, perform a focused exam, and — when indicated — image the joint with on-site MRI or X-ray. You'll leave with a clear plan: conservative care first, surgery only when truly indicated.

## Our approach

We treat the knee and hip as a single kinetic chain. Conservative management starts with image-guided injections, physical therapy and activity modification. When surgery is the right call, we use robotic-assisted alignment for joint replacements and minimally invasive arthroscopy for preservation procedures — both designed to get you back to motion faster, with less tissue disruption.
