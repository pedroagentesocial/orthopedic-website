## What to expect

Joint replacement consultations are unhurried and detailed. We'll review your imaging, evaluate alternative treatments, and — if replacement is the right path — walk you through the surgical plan, recovery timeline and expected outcomes specific to your case.

## Our approach

Robotic-assisted alignment makes every implant placement precise to within a fraction of a millimeter — translating to better long-term function and longer implant survival. Combined with rapid-recovery protocols, most patients are walking within hours, home the same day or next, and back to most activities within six to eight weeks.
