## What to expect

Hand and wrist visits are precise: a focused exam, in-office diagnostic testing (including nerve conduction when needed), and a clear explanation of your options — from splinting to surgery.

## Our approach

Many hand conditions respond to non-surgical care: splinting, hand therapy, image-guided injections. When surgery is required, we use endoscopic and minimally invasive techniques that preserve as much healthy tissue as possible. Most procedures take under an hour and are performed under local or regional anesthesia.
