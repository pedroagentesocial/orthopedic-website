## What to expect

Spine consultations start with a thorough neurological exam and a discussion of how your symptoms affect daily life. Imaging is reviewed in the room — we'll show you what we see and explain it without jargon.

## Our approach

The vast majority of back and neck pain improves without surgery. We exhaust conservative options first: physical therapy, image-guided injections, ergonomic coaching. When surgery is required, we favor minimally invasive techniques that preserve healthy tissue and shorten recovery — typically with same-day discharge.
